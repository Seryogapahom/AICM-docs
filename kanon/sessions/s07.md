### `AICM-L0-PREF-025` — Goal/Program identity and material successor lineage
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §3; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §3.3–3.7
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** Candidate Goal and Durable Program remain distinct durable identities. A material change to outcome, scope, assumptions, dependencies, success boundary, downside or Purpose linkage creates an explicit successor rather than hidden mutation. **Consequence:** object lineage remains auditable across changes and provider/model replacement.
  Draft §3.3 Candidate Goal: «A Candidate Goal is a durable first-class hypothesis that pursuing a bounded direction may improve Purpose realization.» It is not: a command or operator task; an approved Durable Program; execution, capital, legal, credential or trading authority; a Constraint; a Standing Obligation. «The object survives its originating model/agent and preserves origin, evidence and version lineage.»
  Draft §3.4 Goal version and successor: «A material change to expected outcome, scope, core assumptions, dependencies, success boundary, downside shape or Purpose linkage creates an explicit successor version.» Minor evidence enrichment may update the current version only when the semantic proposition remains unchanged. A successor must identify what changed, why the previous version is no longer sufficient and which evidence and obligations carry forward.
  Draft §3.5 Durable Program: «A Durable Program is a promoted, bounded commitment to continue coordinated work under explicit Purpose linkage, evidence, resource assumptions, constraints and applicable authority until completion, failure, retirement or successor replan.» «Promotion grants durable coordination only. It does not grant powers that belong to capital, legal, risk, credential, trading or external-account authority.»
  Смежные object boundaries (не смешивать identity): Task — replaceable bounded unit of work inside a Program; does not independently own Purpose linkage, Portfolio standing or Program continuity. Hypothesis — testable explanatory or predictive claim; Candidate Goal concerns whether an outcome should be pursued, not only whether a proposition is true. Purpose (inherited `AICM-L0-PREF-010–011`, не reopen): enduring constitutional direction; not a current goal, task, ranking or temporary program. Constraint / Standing Obligation — Block 2 фиксирует только отличие от Candidate Goal и Portfolio relevance; exact class/amendment → L0-B3 (`OPEN-06-02` / `OPEN-06-05`).

### `AICM-L0-PREF-026` — Reproducible Candidate Goal minimum
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §3; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §4
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** an eligible Candidate Goal contains provenance/evidence, Purpose linkage, outcome, alternatives, strongest challenge, dependencies/resources, uncertainty/downside, urgency/learning path, exit/closure and distinct outcome-return semantics, plus enough reproducible context for another authorized system instance to reconstruct its basis without the originating model's private context. **Consequence:** persuasive text or model confidence cannot substitute for admissible evidence-bearing content.
  Draft §4 — before a Candidate Goal becomes eligible for Portfolio commitment, it contains at least:
  1. stable identity, current version, predecessor/successor lineage and origin;
  2. originating observation/gap/opportunity and exact evidence references;
  3. explicit Purpose linkage and expected durable outcome;
  4. affected beneficiary-controlled economic value, risk position or capability;
  5. competing alternatives, including `do nothing`, `wait`, repair-first and smaller experiment paths;
  6. strongest available challenge, counterevidence and critical unknowns;
  7. dependencies, prerequisite capabilities and bounded external-enabler needs;
  8. estimated resource classes, reservations and opportunity cost at the semantic level;
  9. uncertainty, reversibility, downside and irreversibility shape;
  10. urgency, opportunity decay, time-to-learning and value-of-information path;
  11. falsification, exit, retirement and Terminal Closed conditions;
  12. expected observability and return path to knowledge, capability, constraints/authority, Portfolio and accountability views;
  13. a reproducibility statement sufficient for another authorized system instance to reconstruct why the candidate exists, what evidence supports it and what remains unresolved without access to the originating model's private context.
  «A candidate lacking the minimum remains in DEVELOP, enters reasoned WAITING, receives an evidence-acquisition plan or is rejected. Persuasive language, creator status or model confidence cannot substitute for the minimum.»

### `AICM-L0-PREF-027` — Autonomous promotion adjudication
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §3; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §5–6
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** promotion to Durable Program is fully autonomous, architecture-defined and role-separated. Neither routine human approval nor one agent owns the decision. Insufficient evidence produces bounded evidence acquisition, reasoned WAIT or REJECT with next wake/trigger. **Consequence:** autonomy is complete at the decision level without becoming arbitrary.
  Draft §5 Autonomous transition governance: «Lifecycle transitions may be fully automatic. Routine human approval is not required.» Every material transition requires: architecture-defined eligibility conditions; current evidence, uncertainty and provenance; competing alternatives and strongest available challenge; applicable constraint and authority evaluation; a recorded reason and distinct responsible logical roles; explicit next state, next wake, successor or closure lineage; an immutable historical decision record. «No human, model, agent, provider or scoring mechanism receives unilateral authority merely because it generated the candidate or recommendation.» When evidence is insufficient, AICM autonomously creates a bounded evidence-acquisition path, defines useful observations and a next wake/trigger and keeps the candidate durable rather than inventing certainty or requesting routine operator prioritization.
  Draft §6 Promotion is a semantic adjudication, not a scalar threshold. A Candidate Goal becomes a Durable Program only when the decision record demonstrates: valid Purpose contribution and no hidden Purpose amendment; sufficient evidence for the next bounded commitment, not certainty about final success; explicit alternatives and strongest challenge considered; comparative advantage over waiting, doing nothing and available competing candidates; feasible bounded resource envelope and visible opportunity cost; applicable authority and constraint path, or a typed dependency request instead of assumed access; observable next outcome and time-to-learning; reversibility or explicit treatment of irreversible downside; falsification, exit, replan and closure semantics; durable continuity ownership at system/program level independent of current workers. «The adjudication is fully autonomous through role-separated architecture. It is not a routine Owner decision and not a unilateral agent decision.»

### `AICM-L0-PREF-028` — Gated multidimensional Portfolio adjudication
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §3; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §7–8
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** candidates first pass eligibility gates and are then compared across multiple decision dimensions. No constitutional global utility scalar determines priority. Current standing is revisable by new evidence/state, while historical decisions remain immutable. **Consequence:** incomparable harms, authority gaps and uncertainty cannot be averaged away or retroactively hidden.
  Draft §7 Evolution Portfolio: «a durable cross-domain model of Candidate Goals, Durable Programs, activated Standing Obligations and their comparative resource context. It is not an operator queue and not a single-agent backlog.» May contain economic, research, repair, risk-reduction, dependency-reduction, observability, infrastructure and architecture directions. Semantic views (not database tables): active commitments; candidates eligible for comparison; candidates in development or evidence acquisition; waiting items with reason, next wake and triggers; challenged, blocked or inadmissible items; activated Standing Obligations; completed, failed, retired and superseded lineage; resource reservations, conflicts and displaced alternatives; concentration and correlated-assumption exposure.
  Draft §8: «AICM must not reduce all Portfolio priority to one constitutional utility number. A universal scalar hides incomparable harms, evidence weakness, irreversible downside and authority gaps and becomes a Goodhart target.»
  Eligibility gates (before comparative ranking): Purpose compatibility; applicable authority path or explicit dependency request; no unhandled terminal or irreversible boundary; minimum evidence and observability; disclosed dependencies, conflicts and critical unknowns.
  Comparative dimensions (decision vector may include): expected durable economic contribution; downside magnitude, tail shape and containment; evidence quality and uncertainty; urgency and opportunity decay; reversibility and option value; time-to-learning and value of information; resource cost, duration and switching cost; dependency reduction or dependency creation; durable capability contribution; concentration/correlation with active programs; legal, contractual and operational feasibility; consequence of delay or non-action.
  Draft §8.3: «The result is not “highest score wins.” The Portfolio records dominance, trade-offs, conflicts, protected obligations and the reason for allocating, waiting, challenging, rejecting or retiring.» A bounded local score may assist within a genuinely comparable class but cannot become the constitutional source of priority. «Current Portfolio standing is dynamic and may be revised when evidence, resources, obligations or World-State/Self-State change. Historical decision records remain immutable; a new decision supersedes rather than rewrites the old one.»
  Draft §9 «No innate priority» — inherited continuation of `AICM-L0-PREF-024` (тела s06 в этой пачке нет; не reopen, не invent body). Session 07 подтверждает revisability + immutability of history внутри gated adjudication, не переписывает 024.

### `AICM-L0-PREF-029` — Opportunity cost, reservations and continuous re-adjudication
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §3; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §10
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** every material allocation exposes scarce-resource use/reservations, conflicts, displaced alternatives, switching consequences and review triggers. Portfolio allocation may be re-adjudicated on material state/evidence change. **Consequence:** local programs cannot silently consume shared resources or freeze obsolete priority.
  Draft §10 — material allocation identifies scarce resources consumed or reserved, including where applicable: capital and risk budget; compute, energy and provider quotas; data access and external credentials; bounded human/external-enabler actions; evaluation and coordination capacity; calendar time and opportunity windows; exclusive instruments, accounts or environments; incompatible assumptions or mutually exclusive actions.
  «Portfolio allocation is continuously re-adjudicable rather than a one-time ordering. A material state/evidence change may alter reservations and standing through a new reasoned decision.»
  Conflict treatment semantic order:
  1. reject or defer action without an applicable authority path;
  2. preserve constitutional and externally binding boundaries;
  3. service activated obligations and bounded risk containment as later classified by L0-B3;
  4. compare remaining candidates multidimensionally;
  5. preserve explicit slack for uncertainty, recovery and exploration;
  6. record displaced alternatives, switching consequences and review triggers.
  «Exact capital/risk envelopes and constraint classes remain outside Block 2. The Block 2 requirement is that conflicts and opportunity cost are visible and adjudicable rather than silently ignored.»

### `AICM-L0-PREF-030` — Bounded adaptive exploration with observable return
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §3; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §11
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** AICM maintains a bounded adaptive exploration envelope rather than leftovers or a fixed percentage. Every material exploration has an observable economic, epistemic, falsification, dependency-reduction or capability return contract. **Consequence:** the system can evolve without permitting unbounded curiosity or economically/epistemically empty experiments.
  Draft §11: «Curiosity is a confirmed property of AICM, but unlimited exploration would consume the system and zero exploration would freeze it around yesterday's capabilities.» Bounded adaptive exploration envelope:
  - exploration is an explicit Portfolio allocation, not spare-time work;
  - it favors high-uncertainty, high-optionality, short-time-to-learning or dependency-reducing opportunities;
  - it prefers reversible experiments with contained downside;
  - it shrinks when critical obligations, recovery, risk containment or resource scarcity dominate;
  - it expands when resources are available, earning programs stagnate, assumptions weaken or opportunity space changes;
  - it cannot starve minimum continuity, reporting or recovery;
  - exploitation cannot eliminate all search for better capabilities, providers or domains.
  «The envelope is not a fixed L0 percentage.»
  «Every material exploration program has an observable return contract. It must produce either economic evidence, decision-relevant knowledge, falsification, dependency reduction or a durable capability result. Immediate profit is not mandatory; an unobservable experiment that leaves no useful economic, epistemic or capability trace is not admissible.»

### `AICM-L0-PREF-031` — Durable continuity, WAIT and Terminal Closed
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §3; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §12–13
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** Candidate Goals and Durable Programs preserve state/cursor, next wake/trigger, dependencies, evidence path, worker/provider fallback, replan/successor, failure, retirement and Terminal Closed semantics. Chat/provider termination is not Program termination. **Consequence:** `Session End != Program End`; waiting is durable and reasoned rather than forgotten.
  Draft §12 continuation contract covers: current semantic state and cursor; next wake time or event trigger; unresolved dependencies and bounded requests; evidence budget and next learning action; fallback if a worker/provider disappears; replan and successor conditions; failure, retirement and Terminal Closed criteria; return path for outcome and residual obligations. «WAITING means durably scheduled for a reason. It is not “forgotten,” “session ended” or “no agent currently owns it.”» A blocked program does not pause the entire system; the Portfolio may advance independent work while preserving the blocked program's next wake and lineage. «Session End != Program End, and provider/model replacement does not erase Program identity.»
  Draft §13 — a bounded Program may reach `Terminal Closed` when: intended bounded outcome is achieved and evidenced; the hypothesis is falsified or opportunity expires; continuation is dominated by a better alternative; required authority/resources remain unavailable beyond a declared exit boundary; downside or constraint conflict makes pursuit inadmissible; an explicit successor assumes remaining work. «Closure records evidence, realized resources, unresolved uncertainty, residual obligations and successor lineage. Failure and retirement are evidence-bearing terminal outcomes, not silent deletion.»
  Draft §16 (stable architectural contract, independent of future algorithms/storage/providers) every Durable Program exposes: identity, version and successor lineage; Purpose linkage; origin and evidence basis; current lifecycle state and cursor; dependencies, applicable authority and resource profile; success, falsification, exit and closure criteria; waiting, next-wake, fallback and replan semantics; outcome/learning return path; residual obligations and Terminal Closed evidence. «Exact schema and storage are not decided here.» (пересекается с inherited `AICM-L0-PREF-023`; не reopen s06 body.)

### `AICM-L0-PREF-032` — System-level no-DONE and distinct outcome return
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §3; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §14–15
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** AICM has no global DONE inside Portfolio logic. After bounded Program closure it returns to Purpose and the next autonomous observation/review cycle. Outcomes return separately to knowledge, capability, constraints/authority, Portfolio and accountability; P&L/local success cannot directly self-ratify knowledge or authority. **Consequence:** individual programs can close without ending system evolution or creating a self-confirming loop.
  Draft §14: «AICM does not reach global DONE merely because all current Programs are closed.» After closure, it returns autonomously to Purpose, current Self-State/World-State and the next observation/gap/opportunity review cycle. «There is always a next meaningful review time or event trigger unless a separately authorized constitutional terminal condition applies.» Such a constitutional terminal condition is outside Block 2 and cannot be invented by the Portfolio or a local agent.
  Draft §15 — every material Program outcome returns evidence to distinct views:
  - **knowledge:** observations and hypotheses strengthened, weakened, falsified or unresolved;
  - **capability:** abilities proven, degraded, repaired, missing or newly acquired;
  - **constraints/authority:** safeguards or boundaries triggered, costly, effective, contested or requiring review;
  - **Portfolio:** realized resource use, opportunity cost, delay, concentration and switching consequences;
  - **accountability:** reconciliable Owner-facing record of actions, costs, gains, losses, uncertainty and obligations.
  «P&L or local success cannot directly ratify a knowledge claim, remove a constraint or expand authority. Each change follows its own evaluation and authority contour.»
  *(HQ reading: «Owner-facing» accountability record ≠ Owner as operator-manager. Reporting contour, не approval loop.)*
