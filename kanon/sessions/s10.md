### `AICM-L1-PREF-001` — Durable Bundle Lineage and Immutable Sealed Edition
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §3; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §4–5
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** Runtime Read Bundle has a durable `Bundle Lineage` and exact immutable sealed `Bundle Edition`. Assembly before sealing is non-authoritative. Material reasoning binds to one exact Edition; authoritative historical resolution through `latest/current` is prohibited. Any post-seal material change creates a successor Edition or an explicitly related Lineage. **Boundary:** sealing fixes content and information boundary; it does not establish truth, evidence sufficiency, knowledge maturity, action permission, RiskGate or execution authority.
  Draft §4.1 Bundle Lineage: durable identity of one continuing governed read obligation. Lineage preserves continuity across refresh, correction, recovery and successor Editions while the stable semantic anchors remain unchanged.
  Draft §4.2 Bundle Edition: exact immutable sealed state of the Bundle at one information boundary. A material reasoning attempt may refer to Lineage for continuity, but its authoritative input must bind to one exact Edition.
  Draft §4.3 Assembly Workspace: temporary, mutable, non-authoritative surface used to resolve references and prepare an Edition. Before sealing: `DRAFT / NON_AUTHORITATIVE`. The workspace is never an authoritative material reasoning input.
  Draft §4.4 Linked learning/outcome records: later records may link outcome, confirmation/refutation, actual use, confidence, correctness or training eligibility to an exact Edition. They are not part of, and do not mutate, the sealed Edition.
  Draft §5.1 identity levels: `Bundle Lineage = durable continuity identity`; `Bundle Edition = exact immutable sealed read state`.
  Draft §5.2 Authoritative pinning: «material reasoning may reference Bundle Lineage for continuity but must bind to one exact Bundle Edition». An authoritative reference to `latest`, `current` or an automatically resolved mutable pointer is prohibited for historical or material reasoning.
  Draft §5.3 Sealing confirms only that: the content manifest and boundary are fixed; the Edition will not mutate; the identity/references are sufficient for reproducible delivery under the declared assembly disposition. Sealing does not confirm: truth; evidence sufficiency for a hypothesis or action; knowledge maturity; action permission; Candidate Goal or Program approval; RiskGate; execution authority.
  Draft §5.4 Successor: any post-seal material change creates a successor Edition or, when stable identity anchors change, a new explicitly related Lineage. Material changes include change to: exact SituationContextSnapshot; included or excluded Foundation releases/assessments; strongest challenge, alternatives, counterexamples or unknowns; applicable Reality Condition, Constraint, Standing Obligation or Authority disposition; capability/dependency/resource assumptions; completeness, omission, freshness, conflict or unavailable-evidence declaration; content needed for reproducible delivery.
  Draft §5.5 Main failure prevented: without exact Edition pinning, historical replay may resolve to later facts, constraints, authority or challenges. This creates hindsight contamination, false learning attribution and retroactive permission or prohibition.

### `AICM-L1-PREF-003` — Professional Governed Composition and Learning-Ready Lineage
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §3; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §7
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** every material Bundle Edition accounts for exact identity/boundary, SituationContextSnapshot, Foundation releases and assessment versions, explicit applicability/retrieval roles, strongest credible challenge, alternatives, counterexamples, unknowns, evidence/source lineage, applicable Reality Conditions/Constraints/Standing Obligations/Authority, capability/dependency/resource assumptions, completeness/omission/freshness/supersession declarations, reproducible delivery and model-independent learning-ready lineage. **Challenge rule:** material challenge is included or its bounded absence/unavailability/non-applicability is explicit; silent absence is invalid. **Learning boundary:** later outcome, correctness, actual-use, influence or confidence records link to the Edition and never mutate it. Training datasets/model artifacts are separate governed derived outputs, not the Bundle or canonical knowledge.
  Draft §7: every material Bundle Edition must account for the following semantic planes. A plane is accounted for either by exact content/reference or by an explicit first-class absence, limitation or non-applicability declaration.
  Draft §7.1 Identity and boundary plane: Bundle Lineage and exact Edition identity; predecessor/successor/related-Lineage relation where applicable; all required binding anchors; exact information cutoff and sealing boundary; declared material read class and consumer scope.
  Draft §7.2 Situation plane: exact SituationContextSnapshot identity; event/availability/cutoff/freshness boundary inherited from the Snapshot; first-class missing, stale, conflicting, unknown, not-applicable and resolution-failed states relevant to the read. «The Bundle references the Snapshot; it does not silently rebuild a different situation from newer data.»
  Draft §7.3 Foundation knowledge and assessment plane: exact Foundation release identities; exact semantic-content and assessment versions where independently versioned; applicability to this Purpose, work anchor and Snapshot; explicit retrieval/inclusion role for each material item; maturity/assessment state without converting maturity into action authority. «Similarity or retrieval score may support implementation, but it is not the semantic reason for inclusion.»
  Draft §7.4 Plurality and challenge plane — for a material read the Edition must include or explicitly account for: the strongest credible challenge; material alternatives; counterexamples and limiting cases; unresolved contradictions; differentiating observations already known; unknowns capable of changing interpretation. Silent absence of challenge is invalid. If no credible challenge was found, is available or is applicable, the Edition must declare that result and the bounded search/availability condition rather than represent absence as agreement.
  Draft §7.5 Evidence and source-lineage plane: each source-derived material must preserve inherited exact evidence/source lineage. Full raw source payload is not duplicated into every Bundle by default. It remains addressable through governed lineage when authorized. Inaccessibility, revocation or unavailability of source material is declared explicitly.
  Draft §7.6 Governance and admissibility plane: the Edition records exact applicable, non-applicable or unresolved Reality Conditions; Constraints; Standing Obligations; Authority Envelopes and consumer read authority. It preserves identity, version, scope, applicability evidence and disposition. Constitutional rules remain explicit: `absence of Constraint does not create Authority`; `Authority does not override an applicable Constraint or Standing Obligation`; `Bundle delivery does not grant action authority`. «The Bundle reports governance context; it does not amend, approve exceptions to or adjudicate that context.»
  Draft §7.7 Capability, dependency and resource-assumption plane: expected capability identity and relevant maturity/health evidence; required external or internal dependencies; unavailable, degraded or unverified dependencies; resource assumptions, opportunity-cost assumptions or budget references relevant to interpretation; assumptions that remain unproven. «This plane records assumptions and references. It does not allocate resources, promote capabilities or create external authority.»
  Draft §7.8 Completeness, omission, freshness and supersession plane: which required materials were included; which were excluded and why; which could not be retrieved or resolved; stale, conflicting, superseded or partially applicable material; coverage limitations and known retrieval boundaries; whether a newer related Edition or Snapshot exists at sealing time; any unresolved issue capable of changing the read. «Completeness means all mandatory categories are accounted for. It does not mean that all desired evidence exists or that uncertainty has disappeared.»
  Draft §7.9 Reproducibility and delivery plane: the Edition contains an exact content/reference manifest sufficient for another authorized consumer to receive the same governed read boundary without private model memory, hidden prompt text, chat history or worker-local state. A model-specific prompt, token layout or serialized rendering may be derived from the Edition, but it is a non-authoritative projection pinned to the Edition. Changing that rendering does not mutate the Bundle.
  Draft §7.10 Learning-ready lineage plane: the Edition preserves enough exact input lineage to support future evaluation, dataset construction, fine-tuning or model comparison. At-seal learning-ready metadata may identify the read class, consumer contour, context boundary, included roles, uncertainty and provenance needed to select the Edition later. Later outcome, confirmation/refutation, actual-use, influence, confidence and correctness labels are separate linked records. They do not mutate the Edition. Under `TF-PREF-029`: `training dataset / model artifact = governed derived output`; not canonical Foundation knowledge; not Runtime Read Bundle. This keeps AICM model-independent and allows future models to be replaced, trained or evaluated without storing system truth only inside weights.
  Draft §7.11 Required core and declared extensions: the semantic planes above form the mandatory core. Purpose- or consumer-specific extensions are allowed only when explicitly identified and pinned to the Edition. An extension cannot hide, replace or weaken a mandatory core declaration. Physical packaging as one document, object graph, addressable read surface or multiple artifacts remains an implementation choice.

### `AICM-L1-PREF-004` — Semantic Assembly Boundary and Advisory Resolver Constraint
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §3; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §8
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** a Bundle becomes a sealed governed read input only after identity completeness, mandatory-plane accounting, information-boundary integrity, governance/conflict integrity and an explicit assembly declaration of included/excluded/unavailable material and fitness for the Read Purpose. **Readiness distinction:** semantics must distinguish fit, fit only for an explicitly bounded/degraded read purpose and not fit; exact labels remain implementation-open. **Authority boundary:** Context Resolver/assembly may resolve, retrieve, assess freshness/applicability, preserve conflicts, declare omissions and finalize a record. It cannot choose Purpose/work, leading hypothesis, truth, authority, Decision, RiskGate or execution. Delivery does not create consumer authority.
  Draft §8.1 Assembly is a semantic readiness decision. A Bundle becomes a sealed governed read input only after five checks: (1) **Identity completeness** — Lineage, Edition and all binding anchors are explicit; (2) **Mandatory-plane accounting** — every required plane is populated or has an explicit first-class absence/limitation disposition; (3) **Information-boundary integrity** — exact Snapshot, cutoff, freshness, successor and supersession state are fixed; (4) **Governance/conflict integrity** — applicable constraints, obligations and authority are not silently contradicted, collapsed or inferred; unresolved conflict remains visible; (5) **Assembly declaration** — included, excluded, unavailable and unresolved material is listed, with a disposition about fitness for the declared Read Purpose.
  Draft §8.2 Required readiness distinctions — exact enum names remain an implementation choice, but semantics must distinguish at least: fit for the declared governed read purpose; fit only for an explicitly bounded or degraded read purpose, such as uncertainty analysis, WAIT or governed non-action consideration; not fit because identity, boundary or mandatory accounting is incomplete. First-class unknown or stale data does not automatically make an Edition invalid. Silent or unaccounted absence does.
  Draft §8.3 Context Resolver / assembly boundary. Within its inherited advisory role, Context Resolver or another authorized assembly contour may: resolve explicit references; retrieve candidate material under declared policies; assess freshness, quality and applicability evidence; preserve conflicts and unknowns; create a non-authoritative assembly workspace; produce completeness/omission/freshness declarations; run semantic readiness checks; emit the exact sealed Edition record when the assembly contract is satisfied. It may not: choose or amend Purpose Interpretation; select a Candidate Goal or Durable Program on its own; create or expand consumer authority; choose the leading hypothesis; suppress a credible challenge because it is inconvenient; convert unresolved conflict into truth; infer permission from missing Constraint; perform Decision, RiskGate or execution; promote knowledge maturity or capability; convert `not fit` into `fit` by preference. «Sealing is record finalization, not substantive approval. Exact actor/service mapping remains open.»
  Draft §8.4 Delivery boundary: the Bundle is delivered only to a consumer already authorized to receive it. Delivery does not create authority. The consumer receives an exact Edition or an exact derived rendering pinned to that Edition. A mutable query, hidden retrieval call or private prompt continuation is not an authoritative delivery.
