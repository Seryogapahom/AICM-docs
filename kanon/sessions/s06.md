### `AICM-L0-PREF-026 (PROPOSED)` — minimum Candidate Goal contract
- источник: register §7; draft §5
- Before a candidate becomes eligible for portfolio commitment it should contain at least: (1) stable identity, version and origin; (2) originating observation/gap/opportunity and evidence references; (3) explicit Purpose linkage and expected durable outcome; (4) affected beneficiary-controlled value or capability; (5) competing alternatives, including `do nothing`, `wait` and repair-first paths; (6) strongest challenge, counterevidence and critical unknowns; (7) dependencies, prerequisite capabilities and external-enabler needs; (8) estimated resource classes and opportunity cost, without requiring exact implementation budgets; (9) uncertainty, reversibility and downside shape; (10) time sensitivity, next useful learning and value-of-information path; (11) falsification, exit, retirement and terminal closure conditions; (12) expected observability and return path into knowledge/capability/constraint/reporting views. «A candidate that lacks this minimum may remain in development or waiting; it must not gain commitment merely because it sounds promising.»

### `AICM-L0-PREF-027 (PROPOSED)` — semantic promotion test to Durable Program
- источник: register §7; draft §6
- Promotion should be a semantic adjudication, not a scalar threshold. Candidate becomes Durable Program only when the decision record can demonstrate: valid Purpose contribution and no hidden Purpose amendment; sufficient evidence for the next bounded commitment, not certainty about final success; explicit alternatives and strongest challenge considered; comparative advantage over waiting, doing nothing and available competing candidates; a feasible bounded resource envelope and known opportunity cost; applicable authority and constraint path, or a typed dependency request rather than assumed access; observable next outcome and time-to-learning; reversibility or explicit treatment of irreversibility/downside; exit, replan and closure semantics; assigned continuity ownership at the system/program level, independent of current workers. «Promotion authorizes durable coordination. It does not authorize actions outside the program's applicable authority envelope.»

### `AICM-L0-PREF-028 (PROPOSED)` — gated multidimensional portfolio adjudication; no constitutional global utility scalar
- источник: register §7; draft §8
- AICM should not reduce portfolio priority to a universal utility number. Eligibility gates first: Purpose compatibility; applicable authority path; no unhandled terminal/irreversible boundary; minimum evidence and observability; explicit dependency and conflict disclosure. Comparative dimensions include: expected durable economic contribution; downside magnitude/tail/containment; evidence quality and uncertainty; urgency and opportunity decay; reversibility and option value; time-to-learning and value of information; resource cost and duration; dependency reduction or creation; contribution to durable capability; concentration/correlation; legal/contractual/operational feasibility; consequence of delay or non-action. «The result is not “highest score wins.”» A local scalar may be used as an implementation aid within a bounded comparable class, but never as the constitutional source of priority.

### `AICM-L0-PREF-029 (PROPOSED)` — explicit opportunity cost, resource reservations and conflict reasoning
- источник: register §7; draft §10
- Every material allocation should identify scarce resources consumed or reserved: capital and risk budget; compute/energy/provider quotas; data access and external credentials; human/external-enabler actions; attention and evaluation capacity; calendar time and opportunity windows; exclusive instruments/accounts/environments; incompatible assumptions or mutually exclusive actions. Recommended conflict order: (1) reject or defer actions without an applicable authority path; (2) preserve constitutional and externally binding boundaries; (3) service activated standing obligations and bounded risk containment; (4) compare remaining candidates multidimensionally; (5) preserve explicit slack for uncertainty, recovery and exploration; (6) record displaced alternatives and review triggers. Exact constraint classes and capital/risk envelopes deferred; Block 2 only requires that conflicts are visible and adjudicable.

### `AICM-L0-PREF-030 (PROPOSED)` — bounded adaptive exploration envelope
- источник: register §7; draft §11
- Maintain a bounded exploration envelope rather than ad hoc spare-time research; allocate to high-uncertainty, high-optionality or dependency-reducing candidates; prefer experiments with short time-to-learning and reversible downside; dynamically shrink when critical obligations or risk containment dominate; dynamically expand when current earning programs stagnate, assumptions weaken or opportunity space changes; never let exploration starve minimum continuity, reporting or recovery obligations; never let exploitation eliminate all search for better capabilities and domains. «The envelope is a portfolio policy, not a fixed percentage encoded at L0.»

### `AICM-L0-PREF-031 (PROPOSED)` — durable WAIT, next wake, replan, failure, retirement and Terminal Closed semantics
- источник: register §7; draft §12–13
- Every Candidate Goal and Durable Program should have a durable continuation contract: current semantic state and cursor; next wake time or event trigger; unresolved dependencies and bounded requests; evidence budget and next learning action; fallback if a provider/worker disappears; replan and successor conditions; failure, retirement and terminal closure criteria; return path for outcome and residual obligations. «`WAITING` means “durably scheduled for a reason,” not “forgotten.”» A blocked program does not pause the whole system. Bounded program can reach `Terminal Closed` when: intended bounded outcome is achieved and evidenced; hypothesis falsified or opportunity expires; continuation dominated by a better alternative; required authority/resources unavailable beyond declared exit boundary; downside or constraint conflict makes further pursuit inadmissible; a successor program explicitly assumes remaining work. Closure must record residual obligations, unresolved uncertainty and successor lineage.

### `AICM-L0-PREF-032 (PROPOSED)` — system-level no-DONE and outcome return into distinct system views
- источник: register §7; draft §13–14
- «AICM as a system does not reach a global `DONE` merely because all current programs close. It automatically returns to Purpose, current Self-State/World-State and the next observation/gap/opportunity review cycle. The only system-level stop boundary would belong to a separately authorized constitutional terminal condition, not to Block 2 portfolio logic.»
- Outcome return to distinct views: knowledge; capability; constraints/authority; portfolio; accountability. «P&L or local success must not directly ratify a knowledge claim, remove a constraint or expand authority.»
