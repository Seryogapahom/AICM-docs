### `AICM-L1-PREF-012` — Governed knowledge presence, absence, discovery, formation and experiment lineage
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §4; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §4, §13; working `L1-B3-01`
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** AICM distinguishes governed knowledge that exists, knowledge not yet available to the consumer, external information not yet discovered/verified, observations not yet formed into governed knowledge, explicit hypothesis/assumption/unknown state and evidence obtainable only through future observation/action/experiment. Missing knowledge triggers an explicit discovery, hypothesis, WAIT, experiment, bounded-uncertainty or governed non-action path; it never becomes an unrecorded fact. **Boundary:** knowledge release/promotion authority remains L1-B4.
  Draft §4.1: knowledge presence is not binary. Distinguish at least: governed knowledge or an exact release exists and is applicable to the current read obligation; governed knowledge exists but has not been released, delivered or made available to the consumer; relevant external information may exist but has not been discovered, acquired or verified; observations exist but no governed knowledge object has yet been formed; the current question is represented only by explicit hypotheses, assumptions or unknowns; differentiating evidence can arise only after time, external observation, authorized action or bounded experiment; the available evidence is materially stale, incomplete, disputed or inapplicable. «These conditions are not interchangeable and must not be collapsed into “knowledge missing.”»
  Draft §4.2: when governed knowledge is absent or insufficient, AICM may discover or acquire candidate information; form or continue a HypothesisCase; request or schedule observation; propose a bounded experiment within authority; enter durable WAIT with next wake and fallback; proceed under an explicitly bounded assumption or uncertainty where the relevant Decision authority permits it; issue governed `NO_ACTION` / `NO_TRADE` when actionability is not established. «It may not silently convert absence into fact, hide the assumption in private model state or present uncertainty as Foundation knowledge.»
  Draft §4.3: a governed knowledge object may move through formation, assessment, release, correction, supersession, retirement and historical retention. Current applicability is a separate assessment against exact context. «Release/promotion authority remains owned by `L1-B4`; L1-B3 only preserves the lineage needed to evaluate use and outcomes.»

### `AICM-L1-PREF-013` — Independent knowledge, uptake, reasoning, Decision, RiskGate, execution, outcome and learning lifecycles
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §4; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §3, §5, §13; working `L1-B3-02`
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** each domain has durable identity, exact input boundary, semantic owner, immutable history and correction/successor lineage. A DecisionTrace links exact records and relations without combining their authority or rewriting the Decision-time state. **Boundary:** reasoning does not imply Decision authority; Decision does not imply RiskGate or execution; outcome learning does not directly ratify knowledge or authority.
  Draft §3 domains remain independent (same physical log may evidence more than one domain; semantic claims stay separate): Knowledge / Foundation release; Runtime Read Bundle; Consumer encounter / uptake; Reasoning; Decision / governed non-action; RiskGate; Execution / non-execution; Outcome; Learning / correction; Learning-data eligibility. None of these implies the next domain’s authority or occurrence.
  Draft §5.1: «AICM must not store knowledge, reasoning, Decision, risk, execution, outcome and learning as one mutable transaction object whose later fields rewrite earlier meaning.» Each material stage has durable identity, exact input boundary, lifecycle and correction/successor path, connected by typed lineage.
  Draft §5.2 DecisionTrace: a reproducible governed view over exact immutable records and relationships. It accounts, as applicable, for: Purpose Interpretation, Candidate Goal or Durable Program; exact opportunity, scope, horizon and authorized Consumer Contour; applicable Constraints, Standing Obligations and Authority Envelope; exact SituationContextSnapshot and Runtime Read Bundle Edition; exact HypothesisCase, active HypothesisInstances and Assessments; alternatives, strongest challenge, counterevidence and material unknowns; exact knowledge and other inputs considered, their reasoning roles and influence disposition; Decision or governed non-action, rationale and uncertainty; later separate RiskGate, execution/non-execution and outcome records. «The trace is not a mutable narrative that acquires later facts as though they were known at Decision time.»
  Draft §5.3: Reasoning may recommend but does not itself create Decision authority; Decision does not own or imply RiskGate approval; RiskGate approval does not prove execution; Execution does not prove success; Outcome learning does not directly ratify Foundation knowledge, remove a Constraint or expand authority.

### `AICM-L1-PREF-014` — Consumer-specific uptake, actual use, reasoning role and material influence
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §4; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §6, §13; working `L1-B3-03`
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** publication/release, delivery/availability, retrieval/exposure, interpretation, consideration, explicit role, actual use/challenge/non-use and material influence/non-influence/dispute are distinct, exact consumer/version/Read-Purpose/context-specific claims. **Boundary:** a receipt proves only the uptake stage supported by its evidence.
  Draft §6.1: every material uptake claim is specific to one exact consumer identity or governed Consumer Contour; one exact artifact/version or Bundle Edition; one exact authorized Read Purpose; one exact encounter/decision context and time boundary; one evidence record and provenance path. «“Available to the system” is not sufficient identity.»
  Draft §6.2 non-collapsible: `published / released → delivered or made available → retrieved / exposed → parsed or rendered into the consumer’s working context → interpreted with an explicit representation → considered for a declared reasoning role → actually used, challenged or explicitly not used → materially influenced, did not materially influence, or influence remains disputed/unknown`. A `RuntimeUptakeReceipt`, where used later, may prove exact delivery, availability, retrieval or exposure according to its evidence. «It does not automatically prove interpretation, consideration, actual use or material influence.»
  Draft §6.3 roles (one or more): support; challenge or counterevidence; alternative explanation; constraint or obligation input; applicability context; uncertainty or missing-evidence signal; planning assumption; disconfirming counterexample; explicit non-use because stale, inapplicable, low-quality, redundant or outside authority. «A negative role is still actual use when it materially shapes reasoning. Mere presence is not.»
  Draft §6.4: material influence may be partial, competing, indirect or disputed. It must state the exact claim: influence on hypothesis assessment, plan formation, Decision choice, non-action, sizing recommendation or another bounded output. «It must not be generalized to “the knowledge caused the outcome.”»

### `AICM-L1-PREF-015` — Typed evidence and prohibition on cross-stage evidence substitution
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §4; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §7, §13; working `L1-B3-04`
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** every material stage and relation requires evidence appropriate to its claim. Evidence of release cannot prove use; retrieval cannot prove influence; Decision cannot prove RiskGate/execution; P&L cannot prove knowledge truth. **Boundary:** explicit observable records and governed assessments are required; hidden cognition is not the durable audit path.
  Draft §7.1 claim → evidence class (illustrative): release existed — immutable release identity, publication record and source lineage; artifact was available — delivery/availability record tied to consumer and context; retrieved/exposed — retrieval, load or exposure event; interpreted — explicit structured interpretation or normalized representation; considered — reasoning record naming the content and role; actually used — linked reasoning operation, comparison, challenge or explicit non-use disposition; materially influenced a Decision — bounded influence assertion/assessment linked to exact Decision rationale; Decision occurred — immutable Decision or governed non-action record; RiskGate — separately authorized risk record; execution — venue/provider/operation evidence and reconciliation; outcome — dimension-specific observation and attribution assessment; learning/correction — linked assessment, correction or successor proposal.
  Draft §7.2 invalid by default: published therefore available to a specific consumer; available therefore retrieved; retrieved therefore understood; understood therefore considered; considered therefore influential; influential in reasoning therefore determinative of the Decision; Decision therefore risk-approved; risk-approved therefore executed; executed therefore caused the observed outcome; profitable therefore the explanation was correct; unprofitable therefore the knowledge was false.
  Draft §7.3: evidence may be direct, derived, declared, independently assessed, disputed, incomplete or corrected. The graph preserves that quality and uncertainty. «AICM must not manufacture proof of hidden cognition; it relies on explicit observable artifacts, governed assertions and independently reviewable assessments.»

### `AICM-L1-PREF-016` — Full immutable Evidence and Decision Lineage Graph
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §4; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §8, §13; working `L1-B3-05`
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** knowledge, bundles, hypotheses, reasoning, Decisions/non-actions, RiskGate, execution, outcomes and learning are connected through first-class immutable typed relationship records with exact endpoints, context, provenance, evidence, uncertainty and correction/successor lineage. DecisionTrace is a reproducible bounded view over the graph. **Boundary:** positive, negative, challenge, non-use, failure, ambiguity and disputed-attribution paths are preserved.
  Draft §8.1: lineage is a graph because one Decision may consume many releases, hypotheses, alternatives, constraints and observations; one knowledge object may reach many consumers; one outcome may have multiple contributing mechanisms; later evidence may challenge earlier attribution. A linear audit trail may be rendered from the graph for a bounded question; «it does not replace the full topology.»
  Draft §8.2: a material relationship is an independent immutable semantic record. It preserves, as applicable: exact source and target identities and versions; relationship type and vocabulary version; scope, context, horizon and temporal order; producer/assertor and evaluator roles; supporting and challenging evidence; strength, uncertainty and disputed status where relevant; reason for assertion; authority effect, normally none unless separately defined; correction, invalidation, supersession and successor lineage. «A relationship does not disappear when it stops being considered current. A later correction or successor changes the current view while preserving the original assertion.»
  Draft §8.3 must also preserve: contradiction; challenge; non-applicability; redundancy; explicit non-use; missing evidence; invalidation; failed execution; null or ambiguous outcomes; disputed attribution; anomalies and counterexamples. «This prevents a self-confirming graph that remembers only selected winners.»

### `AICM-L1-PREF-018` — Semantic validation, multidimensional actionability, governed non-action and separated outcomes
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §4; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §10, §13; working `L1-B3-07`
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** relation instances are validated against exact endpoints, type, context, time, evidence and asserting authority before acceptance. Confidence/actionability remains multidimensional. Decision/non-action, RiskGate, execution/non-execution and scenario/plan/risk/execution/operational/economic outcomes are separate. Corrections create linked successors; P&L is not a direct knowledge verdict. **NO_ACTION boundary:** `NO_TRADE` is a trading-domain scoped non-action for exact opportunity/horizon, not a HypothesisCase state or global stop.
  Draft §10.1: before acceptance a relationship must pass type-appropriate validation: source and target kinds are permitted; exact versions exist and are not silently resolved through `latest`; direction and temporal order are coherent; scope, context and horizon are compatible; required evidence is present and traceable; the asserting role has authority to make that assertion; the relation does not imply an unauthorized downstream action; conflicts, uncertainty or missing prerequisites are explicit. «A relationship that fails validation is rejected, quarantined, disputed or remains proposed; it is not silently treated as fact.»
  Draft §10.2: no single scalar serves as truth, Decision, risk, execution or release authority. Keep distinct at minimum: hypothesis plausibility; current-context applicability; source/evidence quality and freshness; completeness and strongest-challenge coverage; decision usefulness; plan quality; risk admissibility; execution feasibility; operational reliability; outcome-attribution confidence.
  Draft §10.3: a Decision or governed `NO_ACTION` / `NO_TRADE` record preserves exact opportunity and horizon; exact authority context; exact input and challenge lineage; alternatives considered; rationale, assumptions and unknowns; chosen action or non-action disposition; expiry, next wake or reconsideration trigger where applicable. «`NO_TRADE` remains one trading-domain non-action. It does not close the HypothesisCase, halt unrelated work or prohibit capability evolution.»
  Draft §10.4 outcome domains: scenario/hypothesis; Decision/plan; sizing/risk; execution; operational/provider; economic/P&L. «A correct scenario can produce a loss through poor execution or adverse sizing. An incorrect explanation can coincide with profit. Economic outcome is important but not a direct truth function over Foundation knowledge.»
  Draft §10.5: late reconciliation, missing events, disputed influence and revised attribution create linked correction or successor records. They do not mutate the exact Bundle Edition; the original reasoning record; what the Decision knew at the time; the original RiskGate or execution event; the original outcome observation. Current views are derived from preserved history and explicit successor/correction lineage.

### `AICM-L1-PREF-019` — Separate learning-data eligibility and future model-learning metadata
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §4; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §11, §13; `12_TRAINING_DATA_ELIGIBILITY_AND_LEARNING_LINEAGE_GUARDRAIL.md`; working `L1-B3-08`
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** potential downstream use for retrieval evaluation, supervised/preference learning, fine-tuning, distillation or other declared learning purposes is represented by a separate governed metadata overlay covering task, sample role, evidence quality, outcome maturity, rights/restrictions, leakage, contamination, duplication, ambiguity and correction status. **Boundary:** no event is automatically eligible; metadata does not rewrite evidence or authorize collection, training, model promotion or deployment.
  Draft §11.1: learning-data metadata is a separate governed overlay linked to immutable source records. It does not become part of the evidence claim itself and does not alter the original event.
  Draft §11.2 dimensions where a record is considered for a declared learning purpose: declared learning task and intended consumer; candidate role (positive, negative, counterexample, anomaly, failure, abstention, challenge or neutral context); evidence quality, independence and unresolved disputes; outcome maturity and reconciliation status; temporal cutoff and leakage risk; duplication, near-duplication and correlated-sample risk; licensing, contractual, privacy, secrecy and jurisdiction restrictions; sensitive/credential/capital data exclusions; contamination of holdout or benchmark material; label provenance, reviewer/evaluator role and confidence; deprecation, correction, successor and revocation status.
  Draft §11.3: «No Decision, trade, outcome or conversation becomes training data by default. Eligibility for one purpose does not imply eligibility for another. Training-data metadata does not authorize collection, training, model promotion, deployment or use of restricted data. Exact dataset and training lifecycle belongs to `L4-B1`.»
  Guardrail `12_…` (специализация PREF-019, не новый ID) §2: `immutable operational/evidence record ≠ evidence truth assessment ≠ learning-data eligibility metadata ≠ dataset membership ≠ training authorization ≠ model promotion/deployment authority`.
  Guardrail §3: eligibility is purpose-specific (retrieval evaluation but not parameter training; error analysis but not supervised target generation; internal benchmark but not external sharing; negative/counterexample with low sample weight; temporary eligibility pending outcome maturity; permanently excluded for secrets, credentials, contractual limits or personal data).
  Guardrail §5–7: a profitable/successful case is not automatically a good learning example; failures, abstention, disagreement, anomaly or invalidated hypothesis may be more informative when role and limitations are explicit. Winner-only datasets create self-confirming bias. Later outcomes may label a training target only under an explicit task and temporal cutoff; they may not be inserted into the original reasoning input. Operational capture does not imply dataset membership.
  Guardrail §8 does not authorize: collecting new data; using personal, credential, account or restricted information; building a dataset; fine-tuning or training a model; modifying model weights; promoting or deploying a model; using outcomes to bypass Foundation release, risk or change authority.
