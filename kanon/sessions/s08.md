### `AICM-L0-PREF-033` — Semantic separation of admissibility inputs
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §3; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §2–3, §6
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** `Reality Condition`, `Constraint`, `Standing Obligation` and `Authority Envelope` are semantically distinct. A common future technical carrier may not collapse their meaning, lifecycle or authority. **Consequence:** fact cannot be internally repealed, obligation cannot become an optional opportunity and missing prohibition cannot be treated as permission.
  Draft §2 — две ортогональные оси, не одна overloaded taxonomy: Dimension A — semantic role (`Reality Condition | Constraint | Standing Obligation | Authority Envelope`); Dimension B — origin and amendability of a normative boundary (`constitutional | externally binding | internal protective | operational | experimental`). «The two dimensions must not be collapsed. A single technical record may later carry tagged variants, but semantic meaning, applicability, amendment authority and lifecycle remain distinct.»
  Draft §3.1 Reality Condition: «an evidence-linked statement about what is presently true, false, available, unavailable or applicable in Self-State or World-State.» Examples at semantic level: actual capital availability, demonstrated capability, service availability, current contractual state, jurisdictional applicability. A Reality Condition: is not an internal prohibition or permission; cannot be made true by internal amendment; may be challenged as a factual or interpretive claim; may be superseded when new evidence shows that reality or the system's justified understanding changed; must preserve evidence and predecessor/successor lineage.
  Draft §3.2 Constraint: «a negative or conditional normative boundary specifying what is prohibited, capped, restricted or permitted only under stated conditions.» «Every material Constraint names a protected outcome. It is not a Candidate Goal, does not compete as an ordinary opportunity and cannot be averaged away by Portfolio preference.»
  Draft §3.3 Standing Obligation: «a positive normative duty specifying what AICM must perform, maintain, reserve, restore, reconcile or report when activation conditions hold.» «It is not an optional opportunity Candidate Goal. When activated, it enters Portfolio reasoning as mandatory demand and may require resource reservation, a Durable Program, residual obligation tracking or restriction of competing work.»
  Draft §3.4 Authority Envelope: «a positive bounded permission specifying which identity or capability may perform which class of actions, in what scope, under what conditions and consequences.» Constitutional to this block: `absence of Constraint does not create Authority`; `Authority does not cancel an applicable Constraint or Standing Obligation`; `Program promotion creates coordination, not new external Authority`.
  Draft §3.5 External law and contract represented through at least two distinct statements: Reality Condition («the external source exists and is applicable to this subject/action/time/context») + Externally binding Constraint or Standing Obligation («the prohibition, limit or positive duty created by that applicable source»). «A challenge to applicability is not an internal repeal of the external source.»
  Draft §6 effect: Reality Condition shapes Candidate Goal feasibility/dependencies/evidence gaps, changes Portfolio comparative standing and resource reality, may trigger Program continuation/pause/replan/successor/closure when facts materially change. Constraint applies an eligibility gate/condition/cap/prohibition; cannot be averaged away by value; Program must remain within it or use a governed challenge, exception or amendment path. Standing Obligation is not converted into an optional opportunity; enters Portfolio as mandatory resource/conflict demand when activated; may require its own Program, reserved capacity and residual-obligation closure. Authority Envelope: Candidate may exist without execution authority; absence of authority yields dependency, WAIT, non-promotion or bounded preparatory work; Program promotion does not grant the missing authority; execution remains prohibited outside the envelope. «A conflict is not resolved by wording a Candidate Goal more persuasively or by local Program success.»

### `AICM-L0-PREF-034` — Origin-governed amendment authority
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §3; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §4
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** normative provenance determines the change regime. Constitutional/identity, externally binding, internal protective, operational and experimental/temporary boundaries have distinct amendment, applicability, expiry and successor authority. **Consequence:** the component requesting change cannot select the easiest lifecycle; external and constitutional boundaries cannot be locally self-amended.
  Draft §4: `PREF-034` classifies normative boundaries by provenance and lifecycle authority. «Reality Conditions remain non-normative and are therefore outside these five normative families.»
  Constitutional / identity — Semantic origin: Purpose, beneficiary, ownership or other identity-level boundary. How active meaning may change: only by explicit successor through a stronger, separate constitutional authority contour. Forbidden shortcut: same component proposing and activating its own identity rewrite.
  Externally binding normative — Semantic origin: law, regulation, contract, accepted external rule or other binding source. How active meaning may change: AICM records a new external version or changed applicability supported by evidence; it cannot internally repeal the source. Forbidden shortcut: calling internal amendment or exception an external-law change.
  Internal protective safeguard — Semantic origin: internally governed protection of a named outcome. How active meaning may change: reasoned review, challenge, authority-matched amendment or successor with full history. Forbidden shortcut: removing the safeguard because it blocks a locally valuable Program.
  Operational boundary — Semantic origin: normative boundary tied to current operational facts, capability state or environment predicates. How active meaning may change: applicability ceases, expiry is recognized, or a successor is created when predeclared predicates/evidence change. Forbidden shortcut: silent in-place mutation or treating stale facts as permanent law.
  Experimental / temporary boundary — Semantic origin: bounded test, trial, containment or evidence-acquisition contour. How active meaning may change: expiry, review, retirement or successor according to predeclared time/condition triggers. Forbidden shortcut: permanent continuation by forgetting the review boundary.
  «The requesting component does not determine the amendment regime. Provenance and governing authority do.»

### `AICM-L0-PREF-035` — Scope, applicability and evidence separation
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §3; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §5
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** origin, scope, current applicability, supporting evidence and the resulting disposition are distinct and reproducible. No material boundary is globally active merely because a record exists. **Consequence:** false blocking and false permission are both reviewable; uncertainty routes to evidence acquisition, WAIT, dependency request or reasoned non-promotion rather than silent inference.
  Draft §5: «No material boundary applies globally merely because it exists.» For any current effect the system must preserve a reproducible path: `origin and current version → semantic role and normative family where relevant → protected or required outcome → scope → applicability predicates in the current context → supporting evidence and known uncertainty → current disposition / decision and its authority`.
  Draft §5.1 Scope: «identifies the population of subjects, actions, capabilities, resources, domains, jurisdictions, programs, time windows or consequences to which the boundary can apply.»
  Draft §5.2 Applicability: «the context-specific determination that the current case falls within scope and activation predicates hold.» «Scope is stable descriptive reach; applicability is the present evidence-linked conclusion. They are not interchangeable.»
  Draft §5.3 Evidence uncertainty: «Missing or contradictory evidence does not permit silent inference.» Existing L0-B2 paths remain available: bounded evidence acquisition; reasoned `WAIT` with next wake/trigger; dependency or authority request; `REJECT` or non-promotion when admissibility cannot be established; successor/replan of an already active Program when material conditions changed.

### `AICM-L0-PREF-036` — Non-interchangeable lifecycle transitions
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §3; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §7
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** challenge, exception, amendment, expiry, retirement and successor have distinct semantics. Material change creates a recorded version or successor; history is not rewritten. **Consequence:** a temporary exception cannot silently become the general rule, expiry cannot arise from neglect and retirement cannot erase evidence.
  Draft §7 transitions:
  - Challenge — Meaning: questions evidence, interpretation, scope, applicability, protected outcome or effectiveness. What it does not do: does not itself alter the boundary.
  - Exception — Meaning: temporarily changes application for a bounded subject/context/time under explicit authority. What it does not do: does not rewrite the general rule or create permanent authority.
  - Amendment — Meaning: changes normative content through a new version and authority-linked decision. What it does not do: does not erase predecessor history.
  - Expiry — Meaning: recognizes that a predeclared time or condition boundary has been reached. What it does not do: does not arise from mere neglect or convenience.
  - Retirement — Meaning: removes a boundary from active use while retaining immutable history and residual effects. What it does not do: does not delete evidence or lineage.
  - Successor — Meaning: creates a new boundary with explicit predecessor relation after material change. What it does not do: does not silently edit the predecessor.
  Draft §7.1 Review: «`Review` is a non-mutating governance episode.» It may include challenge, evidence evaluation and strongest counterargument, then produce one of: reaffirm current applicability; request more evidence and enter durable WAIT; grant or deny a bounded exception; approve amendment or successor; recognize expiry; retire the boundary; escalate to a stronger or external authority. «Review itself is not permission and does not suspend the active boundary unless an authorized disposition says so.»

### `AICM-L0-PREF-037` — Separation of amendment functions and anti-self-exemption
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §3; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §8
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** proposal/initiation, challenge/evaluation, approval/adjudication and execution/application remain logically distinct. The governing authority follows boundary origin; the constrained or executing component cannot locally approve its own exemption. **Consequence:** autonomy does not become unilateral self-release from constraints.
  Draft §8: the semantic roles remain distinguishable: `proposal / initiation ≠ strongest challenge and evidence evaluation ≠ approval / adjudication ≠ execution / application`. «Any component may propose a review, challenge or amendment request. Making the proposal grants no adjudication or execution authority. The governing decision is made only by the authority contour appropriate to the boundary's origin.» Execution and application are separated from approval. In particular: a component constrained by a boundary cannot locally approve its own exception; a Program cannot treat its promotion as amendment authority; an executor cannot infer permission from the absence of an explicit denial; one model/provider may temporarily perform multiple logical roles only if role identity, evidence, outputs and authority remain explicit and reviewable. «This block does not define the future component topology. That belongs to `L0-B4` and later contract work.»

### `AICM-L0-PREF-038` — Distinct outcome return without self-ratification
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §3; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §9
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** operational, protected-outcome/effectiveness, epistemic and governance outcomes return separately. Any may trigger a review proposal, but none directly changes a rule, grants an exception, expands authority or establishes a knowledge verdict. **Consequence:** local success or P&L cannot self-validate the boundary change that enabled it.
  Draft §9 contours:
  - Operational outcome — What action occurred, whether the boundary was followed and what operational state resulted.
  - Protected-outcome / effectiveness outcome — Did the boundary protect or impair its stated outcome, at what cost and under what context.
  - Epistemic / knowledge outcome — What evidence was learned about facts, applicability, assumptions or causal effect.
  - Governance outcome — Was the authority process followed, where were delays/conflicts and what review proposal is justified.
  «Any contour may create evidence, a challenge or review proposal. None may directly produce its own exception, amendment, authority expansion or knowledge verdict.» `local success or P&L ≠ proof that a Constraint was unnecessary ≠ authority to remove it ≠ proof that the underlying hypothesis is true`.
