### `AICM-L0-PREF-039` — Authority-minimal logical ownership of autonomous-cycle continuity
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §3; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §2–3, §8–10
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** Autonomous Evolution Engine is the logically unique responsibility for coordination, viability and durable continuation of the complete autonomous evolution cycle. Physical realization may be distributed, redundant and replaceable, but competing authoritative control planes are prohibited. **Authority boundary:** continuity ownership does not grant Purpose amendment, Portfolio adjudication, Constraint Amendment/exception approval, knowledge-ratification, Trading Decision, RiskGate or execution authority. **Consequence:** full-cycle continuation has an accountable owner without creating a universal super-agent.
  Draft §2: Engine закрывает continuity gap между иначе корректными, но раздельными AICM contours: Purpose and governed state → material observations, gaps, risks, opportunities and obligations → Candidate Goals / Portfolio / Durable Programs → applicable constraints, obligations and authority → governed domain processes → outcomes, learning, closure and successor state → next autonomous cycle. «The Engine is the logical owner of **continuation**, not the substantive owner of every decision inside that cycle.» Three separations: `logical cycle ownership ≠ physical singleton`; `coordination ≠ adjudication or execution`; `system-wide improvement ≠ domain-authority capture`.
  Draft §3.1: legitimacy derives from already confirmed autonomous goal formation, continuous evolution, operator non-management, durable WAIT/next wake, `Session End != Program End` and system-level no-DONE. «It does not derive from model prestige, provider identity, creator status or unrestricted Owner/Operator delegation.»
  Draft §3.2 Unique responsibility: for every material observation, gap, risk, opportunity, activated obligation, Candidate Goal, Durable Program, WAIT state, dependency, review trigger or outcome the Engine must ensure one of: the correct governed process is initiated or resumed; a durable and reasoned WAIT/non-action disposition exists; recovery is initiated; a dependency/evidence/authority request is recorded; the result is reconciled and routed into the next autonomous cycle. «Material work may not disappear because a chat, worker, model, provider or process terminates.»
  Draft §3.3: `Logically unique` = one authoritative responsibility and one coherent control-plane lineage. It does not require one process, one machine, one model or one agent. Later implementation may be distributed, replicated, redundant or replaceable, provided it does not create competing authoritative cycle owners; duplicate active control planes with divergent cursors; silent transfer of continuity authority to a domain component; dependence on private model context or chat history; identification of the Engine with AICM as a whole.
  Draft §3.4 Authority exclusions: continuity ownership does not grant authority to amend or silently reinterpret Purpose; promote its own Candidate Goal or adjudicate the Portfolio unilaterally; amend a Constraint, approve its own exception or ignore a Standing Obligation; create an absent Authority Envelope; establish knowledge truth, maturity, release or self-ratification; make a Trading Decision, perform RiskGate or execute a trade/action; use local success, P&L or recovery urgency to expand its authority. «The Engine may initiate and coordinate the relevant process and consume its authoritative disposition. It may not substitute for that disposition.»

### `AICM-L0-PREF-040` — Mandatory cycle initiation and recovery triggers
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §3; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §4
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** the Engine must initiate, resume or reconcile the appropriate governed process on due next wakes/lifecycle transitions, material observations/gaps/risks/opportunities, obligation or constraint/authority changes, new outcomes, orphan/stall/unconsumed-outcome conditions, cursor loss/inconsistency and component/provider degradation. **Consequence:** recovery is part of autonomy; a trigger creates coordination duty but not substantive decision authority.
  Draft §4.1: «Recovery triggers have the same constitutional standing as scheduled triggers; recovery is part of autonomy, not an optional operational enhancement.»
  Draft §4.2 Trigger families. Scheduled and durable: due `next wake`; due review, expiry or successor condition; due Candidate Goal, Portfolio or Durable Program lifecycle transition; evidence/dependency condition becoming satisfied or overdue. State and opportunity: material observation or state change; newly detected gap, risk or opportunity; capability gain, degradation or dependency change; resource condition requiring Portfolio reconsideration. Normative and authority: activation, change or discharge of a Standing Obligation; material change in a Reality Condition or applicability evidence; new, changed, expired or revoked Constraint/Authority disposition requiring review of active work. Outcome and integrity: new operational, economic/effectiveness, epistemic, capability or governance outcome; Program closure, failure, retirement or successor event; unconsumed outcome or missing outcome uptake; orphaned/stalled work, missing next wake or contradictory progression; cycle-cursor loss, inconsistency or unavailable coordinator; component/provider failure or degraded operation.
  Draft §4.3 Trigger effect: a trigger creates an obligation to perform coordination, not a right to decide the substantive matter. The Engine must identify the affected durable identity, load or reconstruct the authoritative continuation state, route to the correct governed process and record the resulting disposition and next wake.
