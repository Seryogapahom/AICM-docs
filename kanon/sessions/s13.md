### `AICM-L1-PREF-020` — Consilium Framework as a universal governed pattern
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §4; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §3.1, §4; working `AICM-L1-B4-PREF-001`
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** Consilium is a domain-general governed pattern for plural analysis, challenge and knowledge-evolution recommendation. Trading Consilium is the first domain specialization, not the identity of the pattern. Architecture and other lawful domains may instantiate their own Consilia inside exact competence and authority boundaries. **Boundary:** A common framework does not create one universal super-Consilium and does not merge domain authority.
  Draft §3.1 Consilium Framework: «A domain-general governed contract for assembling plural deliberation, challenge, evidence review, synthesis and recommendation. It defines common invariants and output semantics. It is not one global committee, one agent or a universal authority.»
  Draft §4: «Consilium is a reusable architectural pattern, not a trading-only mechanism. Trading Consilium is the first domain specialization because trading is the first economic domain. Other domain Consilia may be created without rewriting the common constitutional pattern. The common framework standardizes semantics and invariants; it does not merge domain evidence, competence or authority. Cross-domain questions retain separate domain assessments and an explicit authority composition rather than one undifferentiated vote.»

### `AICM-L1-PREF-022` — Dynamic role assembly with mandatory deliberation functions
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §4; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §6; working `AICM-L1-B4-PREF-003`
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** A Consilium is a dynamic collegial process. For each material question it assembles roles appropriate to the task while preserving mandatory logical functions: initiation/proposal, strongest alternative and challenge, evidence/provenance review, applicability and reuse-scope review, use/outcome review where available, synthesis and an explicit recommendation. **Boundary:** No fixed number of agents, models or providers is constitutional; mandatory functions may not be omitted merely because one implementation appears capable.
  Draft §6: «A Consilium may use different people, agents, models, tools or deterministic checks over time. The semantic architecture requires functions, not a fixed roster.» Mandatory functions / required output: initiation/proposal — exact question, candidate, scope, expected value and authority context; strongest challenge — strongest plausible alternative, disconfirming evidence and failure mode; evidence/provenance review — source quality, completeness, freshness, conflicts and temporal cutoff; applicability/reuse-scope review — where the claim applies, does not apply and which consumers are affected; use/outcome review — actual-use/non-use/influence and separated outcome evidence, when mature; synthesis — explicit support, unresolved uncertainty, dissent and recommendation; release adjudication — separate Knowledge Evolution disposition under applicable policy/authority. «One actor may perform multiple non-conflicting low-impact functions only when the policy explicitly permits it and records the resulting independence limitation. Material release cannot rely on an unchallenged one-pass answer.»

### `AICM-L1-PREF-023` — Substantive independence, conflict disclosure and anti-self-confirmation
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §4; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §7; working `AICM-L1-B4-PREF-004`
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** Material conclusions require substantively independent challenge. Independence is judged by evidence access, incentives, role separation, prompt/context diversity, conflict disclosure and ability to preserve dissent—not by role labels or model count alone. The proposer cannot be the sole challenger, evidence judge and release adjudicator of its own candidate. **Boundary:** Independence may be scaled by impact, uncertainty, reversibility and reuse scope, but cannot be silently waived.
  Draft §7: Independence is semantic. It may be supported by: separate role identities and authority contours; different evidence retrieval paths or blind review; model/provider diversity where useful; adversarial assignment and red-team incentives; conflict-of-interest declarations; independent reproduction or deterministic verification; preservation of minority/dissenting assessments; evaluator calibration and historical performance evidence. «None alone is automatically sufficient. Multiple role labels on one opaque reasoning pass are not independence.» The proposer cannot be the sole: selector of admissible evidence; author of the strongest challenge; judge of whether the challenge was answered; release authority; evaluator of the released knowledge’s success; approver of its own self-modification. «Dissent remains first-class. Majority vote, model prestige, confidence rhetoric or economic success does not erase a supported dissenting assessment.»

### `AICM-L1-PREF-024` — Governed self-evolution without privileged self-approval
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §4; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §8; working `AICM-L1-B4-PREF-005`
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** A Consilium may autonomously detect defects in its own framework, roles, policies or performance and create successor change candidates. Those candidates pass the same role-separated evidence, challenge, authority, release, monitoring, rollback and retirement lifecycle as other material changes. **Boundary:** Self-observation creates proposal authority only; it does not create self-ratification, self-exemption or constitutional amendment authority.
  Draft §8: A Consilium may proactively create change candidates for: role topology; evidence policies; assignment and independence mechanisms; tool/model/provider composition; evaluation and calibration; latency/cost/degradation behavior; notification and monitoring rules. «Self-evolution follows the same lifecycle as other material changes. The proposing Consilium does not acquire privileged release or constitutional amendment authority. Accepted and authorized successors progress autonomously into monitored use under `AICM-L1-PREF-010`, with rollback/restriction and outcome return.»

### `AICM-L1-PREF-025` — Multi-level Consilium topology
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §4; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §3.2–3.3, §9; working `AICM-L1-B4-PREF-006`
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** AICM uses a common Consilium Framework, persistent domain Consilia and bounded task Consilia. Domain Consilia preserve long-horizon competence and evolution; task Consilia assemble the exact roles and evidence for a bounded question. Cross-domain work preserves each domain’s separate assessment and authority rather than collapsing them into one vote. **Boundary:** Exact process placement, service boundaries and federation protocol remain realization questions.
  Draft §3.2 Domain Consilium: «A persistent logical responsibility inside an exact domain/competence contour. It preserves domain-specific evidence policies, unresolved questions, performance signals, dissent, reusable knowledge opportunities and long-horizon evolution. Examples may include Trading Consilium and a future Architecture Consilium. The existence of a common framework does not let one domain decide for another.»
  Draft §3.3 Task Consilium: «A bounded deliberation assembled for one exact question, candidate, scope, horizon and authority context. It may be short-lived, but its inputs, role assignments, conflicts, outputs, dissent and lineage are durable.»
  Draft §9: `Consilium Framework → Domain Consilium: persistent domain competence and candidate portfolio; Task Consilium: bounded deliberation for one exact question/candidate`. A multi-domain candidate may assemble a federated Task Consilium, but: each domain retains its own evidence and competence assessment; authority is composed explicitly from the applicable origin-governed contours; silence from one domain is not consent; a common synthesis does not overwrite domain dissent; no cross-domain assembly becomes a universal constitutional authority. «Exact technical federation and scheduling remain later realization work.»

### `AICM-L1-PREF-026` — Class-specific evidence sufficiency under governed policy
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §4; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §10; working `AICM-L1-B4-PREF-007`
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** There is no single universal evidence threshold. Sufficiency is evaluated under a versioned policy appropriate to the candidate class, epistemic claim, impact set, uncertainty, reversibility, reuse scope, affected consumers and protected outcomes. The policy declares required evidence, independent challenge, observation horizon, dissent treatment and permitted disposition. **Boundary:** A Domain Consilium applies the policy and records uncertainty; it may not invent an easier threshold for its own candidate.
  Draft §10: Evidence policy is versioned and selected by candidate class. Dimensions: claim class — descriptive, causal, predictive, normative, procedural, applicability or retirement claim?; impact set — which consumers, programs, capital/risk or protected outcomes may be affected?; reversibility — how quickly and safely can release be restricted or rolled back?; reuse scope — one case, one strategy/regime, one domain or cross-domain reuse?; evidence maturity — direct, derived, disputed, stale, preliminary, reconciled or terminal?; independence — what challenge/reproduction/conflict controls are mandatory?; observation horizon — what later evidence can differentiate alternatives?; authority — which Knowledge Evolution Authority may issue which disposition? «A positive recommendation may remain scoped or conditional. Lack of sufficient evidence yields WAIT, bounded experiment proposal, restricted release or rejection—not silent promotion.»

### `AICM-L1-PREF-027` — Continuous candidate queue with separated knowledge-evolution stages
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §4; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §3.5, §11; working `AICM-L1-B4-PREF-008`
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** Foundation evolution is continuous through durable Knowledge Candidates. Candidate formation/admission, deliberation, assessment/recommendation, release adjudication, dissemination, monitored actual use, outcome return, correction, supersession, retirement and rejection are separate durable stages with immutable lineage and explicit WAIT/next-wake behavior. **Boundary:** A queue item cannot remain indefinitely “promising” without a governed disposition, owner/cursor, trigger, horizon or Terminal Closed path.
  Draft §3.5 Knowledge Candidate: «A durable proposal to create, release, narrow, correct, supersede, restrict, retire or reject reusable governed knowledge.» May arise from: new source/evidence; HypothesisCase/Instance lineage; repeated pattern across cases; actual-use or non-use evidence; separated outcomes and anomalies; consumer dispute or applicability failure; staleness, contradiction or new external conditions; Consilium self-observation. «A candidate is not Foundation knowledge and grants no action authority.»
  Draft §11 lifecycle: `trigger / observation / pattern / correction need → candidate formation and exact lineage → admission/classification/impact set → role-separated Task Consilium → assessment and recommendation, including dissent → independent Knowledge Evolution disposition → release/scoped release/WAIT/reject/correct/supersede/restrict/retire → affected-consumer delivery and successor notification → actual use/non-use/influence evidence → separated outcomes and attribution → monitoring, correction, successor or Terminal Closed`. «Every nonterminal state has an owner/cursor, next wake, trigger or evidence plan. A backlog without continuation semantics is not a durable autonomous lifecycle.»

### `AICM-L1-PREF-029` — Domain assessment separated from Knowledge Evolution Authority
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §4; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §3.6, §13; working `AICM-L1-B4-PREF-010`
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** A Domain Consilium owns evidence-bearing assessment and recommendation about whether a Knowledge Candidate is sufficiently supported, scoped and reusable. Knowledge Evolution Authority owns the governed knowledge lifecycle and issues the separate disposition: release, scoped release, WAIT, rejection, correction, supersession, restriction, retirement or successor request. Foundation history is immutable and affected consumers receive successor/restriction notification. **Boundary:** Neither contour owns Trading Decision, RiskGate, execution, capital, credential or external legal authority. Knowledge Evolution Authority is a governed authority contour, not a routine Owner approval gate.
  Draft §3.6 Knowledge Evolution Disposition examples: `RELEASE`; `SCOPED_RELEASE`; `WAIT / MORE_EVIDENCE`; `REJECT`; `CORRECT_BY_SUCCESSOR`; `SUPERSEDE`; `RESTRICT / SUSPEND_APPLICABILITY`; `RETIRE`; `RETURN_FOR_CHALLENGE`; `TERMINAL_CLOSED` for the bounded candidate. Records policy version, authority basis, evidence cutoff, dissent treatment, affected consumers, propagation obligations, monitoring horizon, rollback/restriction path and next wake where applicable.
  Draft §13.1 Domain Consilium may: identify and admit candidates inside its mandate; assemble Task Consilia; acquire or request evidence within authority; produce assessments, alternatives, dissent and recommendations; recommend release scope, monitoring and correction; challenge an existing release and request restriction/supersession/retirement. «It may not directly mutate Foundation, grant trading/risk/execution/capital authority or treat its recommendation as a release.»
  Draft §13.2 Knowledge Evolution Authority is a constitutionally derived logical authority contour. It may be realized through domain-specific release authorities sharing one common contract; it is not required to be one physical central component. Within its standing mandate it autonomously: verifies candidate completeness and applicable policy/authority; verifies required independence, challenge and dissent treatment; issues the separate Knowledge Evolution Disposition; creates immutable release/successor/restriction/retirement lineage; identifies affected consumers and propagation obligations; monitors actual use, outcomes and correction triggers; routes unresolved or out-of-scope matters without inventing permission. «It does not own Trading Decision, RiskGate, execution, capital, credentials or external legal acts. Knowledge release may inform those contours only through exact Bundle/uptake/Decision lineage already closed in L1-B1–B3.»
  Draft §13.3: «A correction never rewrites history.» Successor/restriction record states: exact predecessor and affected claims; reason/evidence and temporal cutoff; replacement or narrowed scope; affected consumers, Bundle lineages, open Cases, Decisions/programs and future reads; urgency and whether current use must stop, degrade or continue conditionally; acknowledgement/receipt obligations; later outcome and closure path.

### `AICM-L1-PREF-030` — Constitutionally pre-delegated autonomous initiative and authority evolution
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §4; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §14; `12_CONSTITUTIONAL_AUTONOMY…GUARDRAIL.md`; working `AICM-L1-B4-PREF-011`
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** The AICM Constitution and origin-governed derived policies are the primary source of pre-delegated internal authority. Within that standing mandate, Consilium and Knowledge Evolution operate proactively and autonomously: they detect triggers, create candidates, assemble review, adjudicate knowledge dispositions, evolve subordinate internal policies and Authority Envelopes where the originating authority permits, propagate changes, monitor use and continue without routine Owner confirmation. An Authority Envelope delineates autonomous action space; it is not a per-step permission token. **Boundary:** Most future internal expansion is an autonomous successor/change inside constitutionally delegated meta-authority, not “new permission from the Owner.” External participation is reserved for objectively irreducible external rights/resources/actions such as legal identity, KYC, contractual acceptance, payment, account/credential provisioning or another act the system cannot lawfully or physically perform. Beneficiary identity, terminal Purpose, externally binding law/contract and other origin-protected boundaries cannot be silently self-amended.
  Draft §14.1: «The Constitution is not a list of steps requiring Owner approval. It delegates a bounded but powerful internal meta-authority for autonomous goal formation, knowledge/capability evolution, subordinate policy succession, Consilium formation and Authority Envelope evolution where the originating rule permits it. An Authority Envelope therefore states what the system may do autonomously, under which scope, evidence, obligations and stop conditions. It is not a token that a human reissues for each internal decision.»
  Draft §14.2 Proactive triggers: new or corrected evidence; unresolved HypothesisCase or WAIT wake; actual-use, non-use or dispute evidence; outcome divergence, anomaly or regime change; staleness/freshness breach; consumer harm or applicability failure; repeated pattern suitable for Template/reusable knowledge; Consilium performance/degradation; scheduled review or recovery; new Candidate Goal/Durable Program need.
  Draft §14.3 Where constitutional/origin authority permits, AICM may autonomously: create, split, federate, successor or retire Domain/Task Consilia; evolve subordinate evidence and release policies; create/successor internal operational Authority Envelopes; change model/tool/provider composition; activate accepted internal changes, enroll consumers, monitor and roll back; reduce removable external dependencies. «These are governed successors within pre-delegated meta-authority. They are not silent constitutional amendments.»
  Draft §14.4 Exceptional external-enabler boundary: Owner/Operator involvement is required only when an action is objectively external to AICM’s lawful/physical capability, such as: legal identity or beneficiary act; KYC/identity verification; contractual acceptance reserved to an external legal person; payment, funding or account opening requiring the Owner; credential/account provisioning AICM cannot obtain itself; an externally imposed consent or statutory act. «AICM must perform all preparatory work, minimize scope, present evidence/alternatives/consequences, protect secrets, persist continuation and automatically resume after the external act.»
  Draft §14.5 Non-delegable/origin-protected: AICM may not infer authority to: replace the Owner/Beneficiary; rewrite terminal Purpose or constitutional identity without the applicable constitutional successor process; override law, contract or externally binding restriction; grant itself an external legal right; approve its own exemption where origin requires another authority; treat urgency, success or P&L as permission. «This preserves constitutional autonomy without turning autonomy into self-sovereignty outside the Constitution.»
  Guardrail `12_…` §5: «The Owner is an External Enabler, not an internal design or approval service.» Request must include: exact external act and why it is irreducible; governing purpose and authority; minimum requested scope; prepared documents/data and secure execution steps; alternatives and consequences of delay/refusal; secret/privacy handling; persisted continuation cursor and automatic resume rule.
