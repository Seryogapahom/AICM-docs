### `AICM-L1-PREF-005` — HypothesisTemplate, HypothesisCase and immutable context-bound HypothesisInstance identity
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §4; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §3.1–3.4, §4.1
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** `HypothesisTemplate` is reusable Foundation knowledge; `HypothesisCase` is the durable identity of one investigated question and its continuation; `HypothesisInstance` is one exact independently assessable proposition bound to one exact sealed Runtime Read Bundle Edition. Alternatives are separate Instances. Formed Instance content is immutable; assessment and lifecycle events are linked records. **Novelty rule:** an Instance may reference zero, one or multiple exact Template Versions. Untemplated novel formation is allowed with explicit provenance and does not self-promote to Foundation knowledge.
  Draft §3.1 HypothesisTemplate: «A `HypothesisTemplate` is reusable Foundation-side knowledge describing a possible explanatory or predictive pattern.» May include required concepts and applicability; supporting and contradicting patterns; expected observations or path; predictions and horizon semantics; invalidation semantics; known alternatives and counterexamples; differentiating observations; evaluation guidance. Identity: `durable KnowledgeRecord Lineage → exact immutable KnowledgeRecord Version → separately versioned Knowledge Assessment`. «A Template does not assert that the pattern applies now. It is not a live market claim, Decision, RiskGate or execution instruction.»
  Draft §3.2 HypothesisCase: «A `HypothesisCase` is the durable identity of one bounded investigated question or issue space.» It owns continuity for the question; Purpose/work/read-purpose linkage; the competing hypothesis set; unresolved distinctions and critical unknowns; observation/evidence-acquisition plan; WAIT, next wake and recovery; resolution, retirement and closure lineage. A Case survives new SituationContextSnapshots and Bundle Editions; replacement of model, worker, provider or container; session/chat termination; temporary dependency failure; repeated WAIT/resume cycles. «A Case does not own action authority.»
  Draft §3.3 HypothesisInstance: «A `HypothesisInstance` is one exact, independently assessable explanatory or predictive proposition within one HypothesisCase.» Semantic identity includes exact Case identity; exact sealed Runtime Read Bundle Edition; exact claim; target/entity/market or other material subject; material scope; horizon; falsification/invalidation semantics; exact Template Version references where used; formation provenance. «One Instance represents one proposition. Competing alternatives are separate Instances linked inside the same Case.»
  Draft §3.4 Novel and composed Instances: zero-template formation is allowed for a genuinely novel hypothesis and must declare that no approved Template supplied the proposition and preserve formation provenance. Multiple Templates may contribute concepts or reasoning patterns, but the Instance owns the final exact claim. «Template composition cannot silently create or alter the claim. Repeatedly useful novel Instances may later generate a Foundation Knowledge Candidate. They do not promote themselves to Template or active Release.»
  Draft §4.1 Formation boundary: before semantic formation/sealing, an Instance may exist as a non-authoritative draft. «Once formed, its exact claim, scope, horizon, Bundle Edition and Template references are immutable.» Later events are linked records: assessment, comparison, evidence disposition, lifecycle transition, invalidation, correction, revival relation, outcome reference, retirement or closure relation.

### `AICM-L1-PREF-006` — Plural composition, evidence topology and strongest-challenge completeness
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §4; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §3.5–3.6, §5
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** a HypothesisCase owns the competing issue space. Each material Instance independently accounts for exact claim, scope/horizon, support, counterevidence, strongest credible challenge, counterexamples, predictions, invalidation, differentiating observations, alternatives and unknowns. Evidence records remain separately identifiable. **Boundary:** comparative standing, including a current leading interpretation, is a revisable Case-level Assessment and does not erase alternatives, establish truth or create action authority. One aggregate confidence scalar is rejected as the semantic model.
  Draft §3.5 HypothesisAssessment: «A `HypothesisAssessment` is a separate immutable evaluative record linked to one exact Instance and its exact input boundary.» It may state, without collapsing into one score: applicability disposition; supporting-evidence disposition; counterevidence/challenge disposition; prediction status; unresolved conflicts and missing data; comparative standing inside the Case; assessment limitations and provenance. «Assessment is not hypothesis content. New assessment does not rewrite the Instance.»
  Draft §3.6 CaseComparisonAssessment: plural comparison is a Case-level evaluative record covering multiple exact Instances. It can indicate a current leading interpretation, viable alternatives, dominated alternatives and unresolved distinctions. A current leading interpretation is: contextual and revisable; not truth; not a global status of a Template; not Decision, RiskGate or execution authority; not permission to omit alternatives.
  Draft §5.1 Case owns issue space: «The Case must preserve a plurality of explanations rather than one mutable answer.» For material reasoning it accounts for current Instances; known alternatives; excluded alternatives and reasons; unavailable but material alternatives; the strongest credible challenge to the current leading interpretation; unresolved observations that would differentiate the set. «Completeness means explicit accounting, not certainty that every logically possible explanation is known.»
  Draft §5.2 Instance-level requirements: every material Instance includes or explicitly declares absence/unavailability/non-applicability for exact claim; scope and horizon; supporting evidence; counterevidence and attacking arguments; strongest credible challenge; known counterexamples; predictions; expected path where applicable; invalidating observations; differentiating observations; relation to alternatives; unknowns and missing data.
  Draft §5.3 Evidence ownership: «Observations, sources and evidence remain separately identifiable records. An Instance references them and records their semantic role; it does not absorb them into an untraceable narrative.» The same observation may support one Instance, challenge another and be irrelevant to a third; those roles are explicit and assessment-versioned.
  Draft §5.4 No constitutional confidence scalar: «L1-B2 does not use one aggregate number to decide truth or actionability.» Evidence strength, counterevidence, applicability, data quality, temporal relevance, calibration and actionability remain distinct questions. Detailed confidence/actionability semantics belong to `L1-B3`, but L1-B2 fixes the prohibition on using a single opaque scalar as the hypothesis lifecycle or action authority.

### `AICM-L1-PREF-007` — Reassessment, successor, invalidation, revival and bounded closure lifecycle
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §4; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §4.2–4.4, §6–7
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** same exact proposition and same exact Bundle Edition may receive a new linked Assessment. New material context/evidence, changed claim/scope/horizon or material content correction creates a successor Instance. Material change to the investigated question or stable work bindings creates a related successor Case. **Lifecycle:** invalidation is context-specific and preserves history; corrective assessment and context-driven revival are explicit; retirement is not deletion; Terminal Closed belongs to the bounded Case and records residual uncertainty and successor relations.
  Draft §4.2 Reassessment of the same Instance: a new Assessment may refer to the same Instance only when exact proposition, exact scope and horizon, exact sealed Bundle Edition and exact evidence boundary available to the assessment remain materially unchanged. Examples include a second independent evaluator, correction of an evaluative error or a stronger critique using the same governed input.
  Draft §4.3 Successor Instance required when any material element changes, including: new SituationContextSnapshot or Bundle Edition; new material observation not contained in the original Edition; changed claim, target, scope or horizon; changed falsification/invalidation semantics; material Template-Version substitution affecting the proposition; material correction to the original semantic content. «The predecessor remains immutable and the successor relation states why continuation occurred.»
  Draft §4.4 Successor Case required when the investigated issue itself materially changes, including different bounded question; material Purpose Interpretation, consumer/work anchor or Read Purpose change; issue split or merge that changes the comparison universe; successor Program or other work identity requiring a different investigation obligation. «New context inside the same stable question normally produces successor Instances within the same Case, not a new Case.»
  Draft §6 three axes (exact machine labels open; semantic distinctions fixed). Instance record: `FORMING / NON_AUTHORITATIVE`; `FORMED / IMMUTABLE`; `SUPERSEDED`; `RETIRED_FROM_ACTIVE_COMPARISON`. Assessment axis distinguishes at least: not yet assessed; insufficient evidence; applicable or not applicable; supported, weakened or unresolved; invalidated in the exact context; viable alternative, leading or dominated in a Case comparison; assessment failure or unavailable evaluator. Case continuation: active evaluation; waiting for evidence; blocked by a declared dependency; resolved for the bounded question/horizon; retired; Terminal Closed with explicit closure evidence or successor. «The Case continuation state is independent of whether one Instance is currently leading.»
  Draft §7.1 Context-specific invalidation: an exact Instance is contradicted, fails its applicability conditions or meets its invalidation semantics under the exact context and evidence boundary. It does not mean deleting the Instance; declaring the Template globally false; blocking all trading; proving an alternative true; closing the entire Case automatically; rewriting the original Bundle Edition.
  Draft §7.2 Assessment correction: if the proposition and exact Bundle Edition are unchanged but an Assessment is proven procedurally or interpretively wrong, a corrective Assessment may supersede it. The original Assessment remains in history.
  Draft §7.3 Revival is not a silent switch back to active. Two explicit paths: (1) a corrective successor Assessment under the same exact input, when the prior assessment itself was wrong; (2) a successor Instance bound to a new Bundle Edition, when new evidence or changed context makes the proposition viable again. «The successor relation records the revival reason and preserves the prior invalidation.»
  Draft §7.4 Retirement is not deletion and is not universal falsification. Draft §7.5 `Terminal Closed` belongs primarily to the bounded HypothesisCase. Closure records residual uncertainty, unresolved alternatives, outcome/learning return paths and any successor relation. «New material facts create a new related Case rather than rewriting the closed Case.»

### `AICM-L1-PREF-008` — Durable WAIT, persisted next wake, automatic recovery and scoped NO_TRADE
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §4; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §8–9
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** `WAITING_FOR_EVIDENCE` is an active durable HypothesisCase state with exact unresolved question, evidence plan, trigger/next wake, horizon, fallback, cursor and automatic-resume rule. Recovery survives model/provider/process replacement and cannot expand authority or mutate old context. **NO_TRADE normalization:** `NO_TRADE` is a later trading-decision disposition for one opportunity/horizon. It is not a HypothesisInstance/Case status, truth verdict, global stop, system failure or block on capability evolution.
  Draft §8.1 WAIT belongs to the Case: `WAITING_FOR_EVIDENCE` is an active Case continuation state. It is not session end; process sleep; absence of an assigned model; program failure; global system pause; permission to forget the issue. «Waiting normally concerns the distinction among multiple Instances and therefore belongs to the Case rather than one Instance.»
  Draft §8.2 WAIT continuation contract persists: exact unresolved question; active competing Instances and current assessments; evidence or observation needed; why it is differentiating or decision-relevant; time, event, dependency, threshold or composite wake trigger; next scheduled review where applicable; horizon, opportunity expiry and exit condition; evidence/resource budget; preparatory or independent work that continues; fallback and replan path; responsible logical contour; latest consistent Case cursor; automatic resume rule.
  Draft §8.3 Resume path: a wake trigger does not mutate old context. It initiates governed continuation: `trigger or dependency recovery → new observation capture → successor SituationContextSnapshot where material → successor Runtime Read Bundle Edition → successor Instances or same-input reassessment as applicable → renewed Case comparison → continue WAIT / resolve / hand off downstream`.
  Draft §8.4 Recovery: after model, worker, provider, scheduler or process loss, an authorized replacement resumes from the latest consistent durable Case cursor and trigger state. Recovery cannot invent missing evidence; skip successor rules; expand authority; convert a stale Bundle into current context; treat process-local memory as durable state. «A blocked Case does not stop unrelated Programs, Cases or evolution work.»
  Draft §9.1 Semantic owner: «`NO_TRADE` is not a HypothesisInstance or Case lifecycle status. It is a downstream trading-decision disposition for one defined opportunity, horizon and authority context.» For non-trading domains, an equivalent governed `NO_ACTION` disposition may be defined later (OPEN-11-07). Possible reasons include insufficient differentiation among hypotheses; no actionable edge despite a plausible explanation; missing authority, capital, credential or market fact; RiskGate rejection; execution infeasibility; opportunity decay; a better competing use of resources. The reason must be explicit and attributable to the responsible contour.
  Draft §9.3 Non-effects: `NO_TRADE` does not by itself invalidate a hypothesis; close the HypothesisCase; stop evidence acquisition; prohibit future trades under successor context; stop system improvement; block capability promotion; create a global idle state. «A Case may remain in WAIT while the current opportunity receives `NO_TRADE`.»

### `AICM-L1-PREF-009` — Hypothesis authority boundary and exact downstream handoff
- fidelity: verbatim
- источник: `09_…DECISION_REGISTER.md` §4; `02_…PREFERENCE_DRAFT.md` §10; discussion record §9
- текст: **Status:** CONFIRMED. **Decision:** Template, Case, Instance, Assessment and Case comparison remain explanatory/evaluative. They cannot own Purpose/Portfolio promotion, Foundation release, Constraint/Authority amendment, Trading Decision, RiskGate, capital allocation, deployment/container activation or execution. They hand off exact lineage to separately authorized later contours. **Activation clarification:** capability/container activation belongs to later capability proof, change-authority and deployment contours. Those contours are autonomous within standing authority; a human is not introduced as the default activator.
  Draft §10.1 The hypothesis contour may produce: exact Case and Instance identities; assessments and comparisons; evidence-role and challenge records; predictions and invalidation/differentiation plans; WAIT/next-wake continuation state; a bounded explanatory result for downstream consumers.
  Draft §10.2 What it may not do: HypothesisTemplate, HypothesisCase, HypothesisInstance, Assessment and CaseComparison do not receive automatic authority to amend Purpose; promote a Candidate Goal or Durable Program; ratify/promote Foundation knowledge; amend a Constraint, Standing Obligation or Authority Envelope; make a Trading Decision; pass or bypass RiskGate; allocate capital; activate credentials/accounts; deploy or activate a capability, service or container; execute an order or other external action.
  Draft §10.3 Downstream separation: a later decision contour consumes the exact hypothesis lineage and remains separately accountable. Capability/change/deployment contours may later use hypothesis evidence but must apply their own proof, authority, rollback and promotion lifecycle. «The Autonomous Evolution Engine may coordinate triggers and continuity but cannot become the sole hypothesis judge, capability approver, Trading Decision maker or executor.»
  Discussion §9: activation belongs to later governed capability/change/deployment lifecycle (`L2-B2`, `L2-B4`, `L4-B2`, `L4-B3`), not to a human by default and not to the hypothesis object. Within standing authority, the complete lifecycle must remain autonomous.
