## Что такое слой Дугласа (исполнение, не сетап)

Это не разметка, не зоны, не «куда войдёт цена». Обзоры описывают *как жить со сделками*: вероятности, серия, риск заранее, правила жёсткие / ожидания гибкие.

### Пять истин — только формулировки со страниц

**Elearnmarkets** (раздел «A probabilistic mindset… consists of five fundamental truths»):  
«1. Anything can happen. 2. You do not need to know what is going to happen next to make money in the market. 3. There is a random distribution between wins & losses for any given set of variables that define an edge. 4. An edge is nothing more than a citation of a higher probability of one thing happening over another. 5. Every moment in the market is unique.»  
URL: https://www.elearnmarkets.com/school/units/trading-in-the-zone

**Readingraphics** (free page, «5 fundamental truths»):  
«1. Anything can happen. You cannot predict the market. 2. You don’t need to know what will happen next to make money. Just execute your plan and let the probabilities play out over time. 3. There’s a random distribution between wins and losses for any given set of variables. No matter how good the setup, you can still lose. 4. An edge means one outcome is more likely than another—but the outcome is never guaranteed. 5. Every moment in the market is unique. Treat each trade as a new situation, even if it looks familiar.»  
URL: https://readingraphics.com/book-summary-trading-in-the-zone/

**ATAS** (обзор; список *не* озаглавлен «five fundamental truths», это пять пунктов «To start thinking in terms of probabilities»):  
«Accept that anything can happen in the market.» / «Understand that you do not have to predict every single market move to make money.» / «Know that profits and losses are distributed randomly.» / «Believe in your edge, recognizing it as a representation of higher chances of success in every trade.» / «Recognize that every market moment is unique.»  
URL: https://atas.net/blog/trading-in-the-zone-a-book-review-on-trading-psychology/

Доп. ATAS must-read: «it is not necessary to know what happens the next moment in order to make money»; «Your competitive advantage is just a big probability of price movement in a certain direction»; «If you accept the thought that anything can happen, then it is easier to follow the trading strategy even when it does not bring profit.»  
URL: https://atas.net/blog/trading-books-you-must-read/

### Семь убеждений / принципов consistency

**ATAS** («7 Beliefs Essential for Consistent Success in Trading»):  
«1. I objectively identify my trading edge. 2. I calculate the risks for each trade in advance. 3. I fully accept the potential loss of a specific amount on a trade. 4. My actions align completely and unconditionally with my trading edge. 5. I reward myself as the market presents opportunities to earn. 6. I continually monitor my tendency to make mistakes. 7. I recognize the importance of following the principles of consistent success and never compromise them.»  
URL: https://atas.net/blog/trading-in-the-zone-a-book-review-on-trading-psychology/

**Elearnmarkets** («Creating A Belief In Consistency» / «why he is a consistent winner»):  
«1. I objectively identify my edges. 2. I predefine the risk of every trade. 3. I completely accept the risk, or I am willing to let go of the trade. 4. I act on my edges without reservation or hesitation 5. I pay myself as the market makes money available to me. 6. I continually monitor my susceptibility for making errors. 7. I understand the absolute necessity of these principles of consistent success, and therefore, I never violate them.»  
URL: https://www.elearnmarkets.com/school/units/trading-in-the-zone

**Readingraphics free page:** семь убеждений **в обзорах не найдено**. Страница говорит «adopt 2 core beliefs and embrace these 5 fundamental truths that are detailed in our full Trading in the Zone summary» — перечень двух core beliefs и семёрки на бесплатной странице отсутствует (paywall bundle). Не дописывать из памяти.

### Edge на серии / риск заранее / процесс (только обзоры)

- **Серия, не одна сделка.** Elearnmarkets: «At the micro level, the outcomes of individual trades are independent events and random in relation to one another. Nonetheless, at the macro level, the outcomes over a series of trades shall produce consistent results.» Упражнение: «trade at least the next twenty instances of your “edge”». ATAS: казино / «focus on maintaining an edge in probabilities over the long run»; «individual trade outcomes are always unpredictable» (про симулятор replay). Readingraphics: «Trading is thus a probability game with a random outcome… over a large number of events, a valid edge produces a statistically reliable result».
- **Риск заранее.** ATAS: «I calculate the risks for each trade in advance»; «Before entering a trade, evaluate how much risk you are willing to take and only proceed when you can accept that risk without emotional tension.» Elearnmarkets: «I predefine the risk of every trade»; «As indispensable as it is in predefining your risk before opening a trade and placing a stop loss». Readingraphics: «Fully Accept the Risks of Trading»; mechanical stage: «commit to a system with a clearly defined edge—including entry, stop-loss, and time frame».
- **Процесс, не P&L последней сделки.** Readingraphics quote на странице: «If your goal is to trade like a professional and be a consistent winner, then you must start from the premise that the solutions are in your mind and not in the market.» ATAS: «When you have accepted the risk, you will be at peace with any outcome.» Elearnmarkets: «Most traders virtually live or die (emotionally) on the results of the most recent trade.»
- **Новости как правило входа/игнора:** в трёх обязательных обзорах **операционального правила нет**. ATAS/Readingraphics критикуют *фундаментальный анализ как достаточность* («does not account for the behavior of other traders» / «market prices are driven by irrational perceptions»), но не пишут «не читай новости» и не пишут «новость → вход». Не выдумывать.

## Не гибрид

Это **не** гибрид «волны+зона».

Слой Дугласа в этой сверке — психология вероятностного *исполнения*: пять истин (как их печатают обзоры), семь убеждений (ATAS + Elearnmarkets; Readingraphics free — не нашла), edge на серии, риск заранее. Обзоры не дают ценовых зон, фибо-коридоров, наклонок и не заменяют сетап входа.

Не склеивать с **узким S-1** (сетап №1) в «волны, но по Дугласу в зонах». Не склеивать со **штабовским** пакетом Андреева и с **D-1** Радченко в одну механику «и волны, и новость, и зона». Traffic Light / 2.27 / сетки — не S-1 и не Дуглас. EWP hard rules — про *счёт импульса*, не про «как не сломаться на серии». Три provenance гостей и вебинар 2024 не сливать.

Consilium может держать Дугласа *рядом* как кандидат исполнения поверх *уже выбранного* edge. Это не третий вход и не канон.
