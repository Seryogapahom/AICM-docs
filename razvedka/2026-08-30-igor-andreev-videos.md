## Who this Andreev is

Игорь Андреев (brand **Igor Kripta / Igor_Kripta**) is a Russian-speaking crypto/futures trader and educator who publicly teaches **Elliott Wave + technical analysis + proprietary indicators**, and who sells the paid **«Школа трейдинга»** on [school.igorkripta.ru](https://school.igorkripta.ru). His own YouTube about-box and Telegram bio present the Telegram channel [@igorcryptotrader](https://t.me/igorcryptotrader) as the **only official** wave-analysis channel; school legal entity is **ИП Андреева Татьяна Геннадьевна** (ИНН 246304981526, educational licence №Л035-01211-24/01008356 dated 2023-12-27) per the school footer and secondary reviews.

In interviews he says he is from **Krasnoyarsk**, previously worked as a TV cameraman, started trading around **2007** (he has also marketed «15 years»; one review cites a public claim of trading since 2010), later lived on **Bali**, and by 2026 was appearing on Moscow/Dubai podcasts. On the Hedlainery podcasts (Mar 2026) he is introduced as trading Elliott Wave and, **by his own claim**, running **1,200+ Bybit trades from Apr 2024** with a working deposit described as **$100k** and an equity peak described as **$400k**. Those P&L figures are **self-reported on podcasts**, not independently audited in this catalog.

Sources: [YouTube @igor_kripta](https://www.youtube.com/@igor_kripta), [t.me/igorcryptotrader](https://t.me/igorcryptotrader), [school.igorkripta.ru](https://school.igorkripta.ru), Hedlainery episodes [AodqaoVPLOY](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AodqaoVPLOY) / [podcast.ru/e/3VK5oo5J6rN](https://podcast.ru/e/3VK5oo5J6rN), Boris Zdor interview [_UT2OYJXrJI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_UT2OYJXrJI), review pages [coinspot.io](https://coinspot.io/rejting-trejderov/igor-andreev-otzyvy/) and [coinmania.com](https://coinmania.com/rejting-luchshikh-trejderov/kanal-telegram-igor-andreev-treiding-volnovoi-analiz-igor-andreev-team-realnye-otzyvy-razvod/).
